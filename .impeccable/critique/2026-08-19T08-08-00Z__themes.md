#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The theme architecture is well-structured with 3 distinct chromatic identities (Cobalt Blue, Ocean Cyan, Amethyst Twilight) and 2 appearance modes. However, deep inspection reveals token leakage: multiple UI components still rely on hardcoded `zinc` and `white` utilities rather than semantic theme tokens (`bg-app-card`, `bg-app-card-subtle`, `border-app-border`), diluting the immersion of Ocean and Amethyst in dark mode.

**Deterministic scan**: Detector scan passed cleanly (`0 findings`), confirming no structural CSS anti-patterns or gray-on-color regressions.

#### Overall Impression
The multi-theme system adds impressive character and personalization. Resolving the white-on-cyan button contrast in Ocean Dark and unifying hardcoded zinc card borders with theme tokens will make the theme system feel unified and professional.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Ocean Dark Primary Button Contrast**: In Ocean Dark, `--brand-color: #06b6d4` with `--brand-fg: #ffffff` yields a contrast ratio of ~2.5:1 (failing WCAG AA 4.5:1). `--brand-fg` should be dark (`#040d16`) for cyan-500, or `--brand-color` adjusted to cyan-600 (`#0891b2`) with white text.
- **[P1] Hardcoded Zinc Card Backgrounds & Borders**: Components (Editor, SettingsModal, ConfigVisualizer) hardcode `bg-zinc-100` / `bg-zinc-900` / `border-zinc-200` instead of using `--bg-card`, `--bg-card-subtle`, and `--border-app`, which breaks the tinted ambiance of Ocean (`#040d16`) and Amethyst (`#0a0612`).
- **[P2] Settings Modal Swatch Asymmetry**: Ocean theme swatch uses `bg-cyan-500` (lighter) while Default and Amethyst use `600` weight (`bg-blue-600`, `bg-purple-600`), causing uneven visual weight in the settings selector.
- **[P2] Scrollbar & Selection Theming**: Scrollbar thumbs remain neutral gray across all 3 themes rather than inheriting subtle theme chroma.

#### Persona Red Flags
- **Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)**: Cannot read white text on primary buttons when Ocean theme is active in dark mode due to insufficient luminance contrast.
- **Riley (Visual Stress Tester)**: Notices neutral gray cards and input borders popping against the deep purple background of Amethyst Dark.

#### Minor Observations
- Role badge `fast` in ConfigVisualizer hardcodes indigo (`bg-indigo-100 text-indigo-800`), which clashes with Ocean Cyan theme.
